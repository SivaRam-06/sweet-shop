Our Story

Sweet Shop was born from a passion for traditional Indian sweets and a dream to bring the finest handcrafted confections to every doorstep. What started as a small family kitchen in Mumbai has grown into a beloved online destination for sweet lovers across the country.

Every sweet we make is prepared fresh daily using time-honored recipes passed down through generations, combined with the finest ingredients sourced from trusted suppliers.
